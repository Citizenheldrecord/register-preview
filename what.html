What this is

This is a citizen's dated record of what governments promised and whether they delivered. Each promise is written down before the result is known: what must happen, by what date, and who answers for it. On that date it is checked against the official record and marked passed or failed. One law, one ledger: the same rule for every promise, and nothing is erased.

The full counts, with their definitions, are on the register page.
